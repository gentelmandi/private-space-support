Help and support

For help with Gently Forward, email gentelmandi@gmail.com. The publisher is Mobdi. Please include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Do not send your journal, children’s information, device passcode or biometric details.

There is no user account or cloud recovery. Most features work offline. External searches, websites and this support page need internet.

Privacy and app lock

Open Settings > Privacy and data. Settings also includes direct Privacy policy, Help & support and Website links under About. The public policy and contact remain available under Privacy information. Optional app lock uses the device’s own authentication; we cannot unlock the device or recover its encryption key.

Notifications

Reminders begin disabled. Enable them explicitly in the app and allow notifications in iOS Settings. Focus modes and device settings can affect delivery. You can pause reminders or turn them off. The core app remains usable without notification permission.

Sleep audio and guided activities

Start audio only when you want to listen. Sleep recordings are bundled with the app. Use pause/stop and device media controls. Guided movement voice pauses when you leave the app. If audio stops after an interruption or headphones disconnect, resume only when you are ready. Stop or adapt movement if you feel pain, dizziness or increased distress.

Exporting or deleting your data

Privacy and data includes Export for my records and Delete all my data. Exports are readable and cannot be imported to restore the app. Store them safely. Deleting app data does not remove copies you previously shared with other services or recipients.

Advice and stories

Only cleared readings are available in the public-release configuration. A reading collection is not offered on Home while no readings are published. Development previews do not establish publication rights or professional approval.

Unavailable links and search results

External services may change their coverage, terms or availability. Review and edit search words before sending them to a search engine. We do not operate or moderate external communities. Report a broken app link by email without including personal search details.

Lifetime purchases and optional ads

Open Settings → Lifetime access to purchase or restore non-expiring access through Apple. Use the same Apple Account as the original purchase. The App Store shows your local price. Restoration recovers paid access, not deleted personal records. Pending purchases unlock only after Apple approval. If the store is temporarily unavailable, existing verified rights are kept.

Three advanced uses per day are free across spaces. Optional ads can earn up to two extra uses per day. Counters renew at local midnight; unused free uses do not accumulate. An earned extra use remains until successfully used, with one unspent reward at a time. No ad availability is guaranteed. Writing, existing records and privacy tools remain free. Use Settings → Advertising privacy and ad reporting to change required privacy choices or report an ad. Include only the ad description and approximate time; do not send personal journal content.

Wellbeing and urgent situations

Gently Forward is not medical treatment, psychotherapy, legal advice or an emergency response service. Nobody monitors your entries. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a suitable local professional directly. This support mailbox is not continuously monitored for urgent help.

The app is currently in pre-release development; professional content review and physical iPhone validation remain necessary before public release.
