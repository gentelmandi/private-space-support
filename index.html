Your private space, at your pace

Information and support for Separation & Divorce: Move On, a personal wellbeing app for adults navigating separation or divorce.

Your writing stays in an encrypted database on your device. There is no user account, developer analytics service or automatic cloud sync. Optional Google AdMob rewarded ads provide extra advanced uses; an optional Apple lifetime purchase removes usage limits and ads. Personal writing is never included in ad requests.

The current app is in pre-release development. This site does not claim App Store availability, clinical approval or a completed professional content review.

Separation & Divorce: Move On is a self-help tool. It is not a medical, psychotherapy, legal or emergency service. Nobody monitors your journal.
