Privacy policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026. Applies to the current Gently Forward app and these information pages.

The current app stores the personal information you choose to enter on your device. There is no user account, developer backend or developer analytics service. Optional rewarded advertising is provided by Google AdMob, and Apple processes the optional lifetime purchase. Your journal is not monitored.

Who is responsible and how to contact us

Gently Forward is published by Mobdi. For privacy questions, email gentelmandi@gmail.com. Please do not email journal entries, information about children or other sensitive personal details. This mailbox is not an urgent support service.

Information stored on your device

The app can store your optional relationship and family context, needs, feelings, preferences, journal entries and drafts, activity history, saved support, feedback and reminders. It also stores optional audio preferences and favourites, activity checkpoints, manually chosen cities/countries/destinations, search preferences and links you choose to save. No child names or birth dates are requested. Your answers help tailor your chosen activities; they are not a diagnosis.

These records are kept in a local encrypted database. Its encryption key is held in device secure storage. The app excludes its private database directory from automatic device backup and does not provide cloud sync or recovery. It does not request GPS, microphone, camera, photo-library or contact access for its current features.

Notifications, authentication and audio

Reminders are local and initially disabled. Permission is requested when you choose to enable them. Notification previews can use discreet wording. Your journal text and profile details are not inserted in notification messages; the operating system may still show the app name and icon.

Optional app lock uses device authentication. The app does not receive biometric templates. Included sleep recordings play locally. Optional guided speech uses the device speech system. Neither feature records your voice or sends your writing to an AI service.

External searches and websites

Only an explicit action opens a search or external website. Connect searches can include the need, separation/divorce situation, language and area you select, together with words you edit yourself. Explore searches send the place type, chosen city/country and, where relevant, company preference to Google Maps or Google Search. Connect can use Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Baidu. The app does not automatically attach journal entries or your private session history.

The search engine, website or associated app receives the URL/search terms and can process device/network information such as your IP address under its own terms. Review the words before opening a search and avoid including sensitive personal details. We do not control those services or their retention practices. The app has no background analytics collection.

Optional advertising and lifetime purchases

Three advanced actions per day are free across the app. You may choose to view an advertisement to earn one extra use, up to two rewards per day. Essential features and access to existing personal records remain available without viewing ads. Earned uses are stored locally until used. Usage counters and unlocked-comparison references are stored separately from personal records and are not reset by deleting journal records. Apple purchase rights are managed by your Apple Account; deleting personal records does not cancel a purchase.

Google AdMob and its consent platform can process IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device information, advertising interactions, consent choices and diagnostics. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables the publisher first-party identifier and does not request cross-app tracking permission. It does not send journal entries, feelings, relationship context, chosen areas or other private content to advertising services. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

Advertising privacy choices and an option to report inappropriate ads are available from Settings → Advertising privacy and ad reporting. Required privacy messages depend on your region. Google handles advertising and consent records under its own retention policies; see Google privacy policy and Google advertising information. Changing privacy choices does not erase records already held by Google; use Google's privacy controls for applicable requests. Required consent is checked before ad requests.

Lifetime access is a non-expiring, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase with a reference price of USD 19.99 and local App Store pricing. It removes usage limits and advertising. Apple processes payment; the app does not receive payment card details. StoreKit verifies purchase rights on the device, including restoration and revocation. See Apple privacy policy. Restoring an Apple purchase restores access, not journal records.

Export and sharing

Export is voluntary. It creates a readable JSON file and opens the system sharing sheet. The file contains historical records supported by the export, and is not a complete backup of every feature preference. Import and restoration are not available. A destination you choose can store or transmit the file. Keep the file private; recipients and export copies are outside the app lock and local encryption.

Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them. You can remove individual journal entries or use Privacy and data → Delete all my data to erase local personal records and cancel reminders. Previously exported, emailed or shared copies remain with the destination or recipient; deleting app records does not delete those copies. Removing the app or losing access to your device may lose your records. No developer server holds a recovery copy.

Support emails

If you contact us, your email and the details you send are processed through the email provider to answer your request. Send only what is necessary, such as the app/iOS version and a description without private content. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Adults and wellbeing content

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is a self-help tool, not medical treatment, psychotherapy, legal advice or an emergency service. The current pre-release content still requires professional review before public release. No clinical approval is claimed.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub may process request information to operate and secure its service under the GitHub privacy statement. Contact links open your email application only when selected.

Changes

This policy describes the current implementation. We will update this page and its date when the app or its data handling changes.
